F64

Unlock the full potential of your mobile camera

A pure-sensor manual camera for Android. F64 strips away the stock camera's processing pipeline and gives you the sensor's raw output with full manual control.

  • Photos — RAW (DNG) capture, or minimal-processing JPEG
  • Video — manual-settings recording, no OEM post-processing
  • Controls — ISO, shutter speed, focus, white balance, flash
  • Smart auto — light- and shake-aware automatic exposure
