Privacy Policy for F64

Last updated: 22 August 2026

F64 (com.camera.flash) is a manual camera app for Android. This policy describes what the app uses and why.

What the app does

F64 captures photos and video on your device. It does not create accounts or run its own servers for your media.

Data stored on your device

Photos and videos are saved in the device media library (DCIM / Movies). They stay on the phone unless you share them with another app.

Permissions

  • Camera — capture photos and video
  • Microphone — record audio with video
  • Internet — load ads
  • Photos/storage (older Android) — save and show captures

Advertising

The gallery may show ads from Google AdMob. Google and its partners may use device information such as Advertising ID, IP address, device type, and ad interactions to serve and measure ads.

See Google's privacy policy.

Europe (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are in these regions, a consent form may appear before personalized ads.

To change or withdraw that consent later, open F64, go to the gallery, and tap Privacy options. That re-opens the consent form. You can also uninstall the app.

United States

If a US-state privacy message is shown, you can opt out of the sale/share of personal information used for ads.

Children

F64 is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Your choices

You can turn off Advertising ID in Android settings, deny optional permissions, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app; media already saved in the gallery remains on the device.
